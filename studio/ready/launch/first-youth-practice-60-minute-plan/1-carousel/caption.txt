Your first practice: a dozen kids, a bag of cones, and about an hour. Here is what I would run.

Get there 15 minutes early. Set up two play areas with a ball at every spot. Kids walk into something to do while you learn names. That is the first eight minutes.

Then, roughly:

4 minute huddle. Names again. One rule.
Tag game to warm up. Not a lap. Nobody got better running a lap.
One skill, taught in under a minute, then run as a game.
Two short games, then 3v3.
Take a knee. Ask: are we perfect? What can we do to keep getting better?

Open loud ("who's ready to run like crazy people?") and close on a question.

Write it on an index card. Expect it to go sideways by minute twenty. A plan that bends still beats a coach making it up in front of twelve kids.
