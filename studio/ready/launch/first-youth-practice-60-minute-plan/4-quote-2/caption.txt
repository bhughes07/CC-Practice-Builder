Warm up with a game, not a lap.

Nobody got better jogging around a field, and the kid at the back of the lap is learning that practice is a punishment.

Play tag. They are warm in three minutes, they are laughing, and you got a look at who can move. Lines and laps are where waiting kids learn nothing.
