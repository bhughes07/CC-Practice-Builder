Should you give equal playing time? No. Here is how I actually weigh it.

1. Read the rulebook first. Your league already set a floor. Little League's mandatory play rule, AYSO's half-of-every-game floor and travel ball each promise something different, and none of them promise equal. Whatever you write has to clear your league's floor.

2. Know your team. Five-year-olds in rec: equal is easy and right. Eleven-year-olds who tried out and drive an hour each way: harder, and I will not pretend otherwise.

3. You can play to win and still develop every kid. Find the spot where each one succeeds and get as many in the game as you can.

4. Write it down. Keep it short, read it at the parent meeting, hand out copies, and ask parents to wait 24 hours before any complaint.

Nothing good has ever come out of a parking lot argument. The policy on paper is how you avoid one.
