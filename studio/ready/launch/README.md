# Launch set: 20 posts, reworked

The October kits from `cheat-code-coaching/social/ready/` redone for the empty Instagram and
Facebook grid. Same slugs and folder numbers as the originals; reels are omitted (Brad films
those). Every folder holds the images (1080x1350 JPEG, sRGB) and a `caption.txt` that ends with
the fixed CTA and up to eight hashtags. Nothing here has been posted; Brad approves each post.

Renderer: `studio/scripts/social/render-launch.js`, copy and photo picks in
`studio/scripts/social/launch-posts.json`. Working files and contact sheets in
`studio/renders/launch/` (`sheets/all-covers.jpg` is every post's first slide; `sheets/grid-first-9.jpg`
mocks the grid after the first nine go up).

## What changed across the set

- One system for every carousel: photo cover, index card on night for the beats, the fixed CTA
  slide last. The Paper-style type-only slides are gone, so the League and Build log carousels
  no longer look like a different brand from the Parents ones.
- Legibility at phone size: body copy 42px on the cards (was 38 to 42 with a 400px dead gap
  above it), 36px cover subtitles (was 30), the series eyebrow is now a blue chip at 26px
  instead of 20 to 22px tracked caps, slide counters and the footer question are 26px.
- The mark's cut corner (from the brand guide) is on the chip, the card and the phone frame.
- Screenshot slides: headline and body on top, the demo-org screen large and bleeding off the
  bottom, so the part of the screen that matters is readable. Org Suite desktop shots are cropped
  to the content panel from the 1800px originals instead of a 936px thumbnail of the whole page.
- Every post has its own photo. The old kits repeated the same five photos across different
  posts (the first-practice cover, the "what it is" cover and product quote 1 were the same
  frame). Sports rotate so no two consecutive posts share one.
- Covers get a darker top band so the chip and mark hold up on bright skies.
- Captions rewritten in Brad's voice: a first line that stands on its own, the point taught in the
  caption, no duplicated blocks (the old carousel captions were pasted twice), quote captions no
  longer repeat the carousel summary verbatim. No em dashes anywhere.
