Nobody at the game should be able to tell which kid is mine. I miss that more often than I would like.

Four things that help when you coach your own child:

1. Parent first. You had that job before they ever played a down, and you still have it after a loss.

2. One standard. Riding your own kid harder so the bleachers notice is still favoritism, just pointed the other way.

3. The ride home is not film study. "I liked watching you today. Where do you want to eat." If they want to replay the game, they will bring it up.

4. Save the skills talk for practice. That is where it belongs, and where the rest of the team hears it too.
