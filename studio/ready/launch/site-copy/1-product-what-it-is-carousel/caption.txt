One app for the whole season, built by a coach who was tired of running five.

Here is what is in Cheat Code Teams:

A development card for every player. Season plan, weekly tasks, game reports. Their parents see it. Nobody else does.

Practice Builder. Drag drills from a real library into a timed plan and know when practice ends before you leave the house.

Your playbook in every parent's hands, next to the calendar, the chat and their own kid's progress. One app they will actually open.

One parents chat and one coaches chat, with topics, so the snack schedule stops burying the field change.

A draft board.

I am a youth football head coach in Trophy Club, Texas, with a full-time job and a family. I coach with this every week, and it works for any sport.

Nobody gets skipped. Sign up as a coach at cheatcodecoaching.app, then download Cheat Code Teams on the App Store.
