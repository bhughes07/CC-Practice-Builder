A messaging app, a scheduling app, practice plans in a notes app, and a notebook in the truck.

That was my setup, and I bet it looks like yours. Four problems with it:

1. Whoever built those apps never installed a new play on Tuesday and needed parents to have it by Thursday.

2. Practice is short and you have a full-time job. Developing every player should be the easy part. It was the part that got skipped.

3. Five screens to find anything. Coaches and parents should never hunt.

4. Coaching is mostly communication. When the tools are bad, the coaching suffers and the team never plays to what it could be.

So we fixed the tools. One app, one design, the same for every coach and every parent.
