I took a team with my son on it and we won it all. The trophy is not the part that stuck with me.

What stuck: our season was never going to be decided by my best players. It hinged on whether the rest of the roster got better.

And I was coaching those kids in spite of my tools, not because of them. I paid for the apps everyone uses and still hand-typed the schedule into my phone after work. That hour belonged to the kid who could take a step that month.

So I built the app I needed. Draft board, Practice Builder, one chat, and a development plan for every player.

I have a full-time job and a family, and I still coach with it every week. Built in that life, by someone in it.
